Hi, I'm Akshita

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm a graphic designer and visual storyteller with a deep passion for creating modern, functional, and aesthetically pleasing digital experiences.

This blog serves as a dedicated space to document my journey through the ever-evolving world of design. From the intricate details of typography to the broad concepts of brand identity, I love dissecting what makes a design truly resonate with its audience.

Here, I share my thoughts on pushing boundaries in UI/UX, the importance of inclusive design, and how to balance creativity with usability. My goal is to provide you with insights that bring clarity and elegance to your own design projects.

When I'm not pushing pixels or writing about design, you can find me exploring art galleries, sketching new illustration ideas, and discovering new coffee shops to fuel my creative process.

I hope you find something here that inspires your next creative endeavor.
